Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 29, 2026

Social Calendar (“we”, “us”) helps you manage contacts, events, and relationship reminders. This policy explains what we collect and how we use it. The service is operated via join-social.com and related app stores.

Information we collect

  • Account data — email, name, password (stored hashed), and optional profile details you provide (phone, birthday, location, organization, profile photo).
  • Contacts & social data — people you add, notes, tags, interaction logs, and events you create.
  • Usage & device data — basic technical logs needed to run and secure the service (for example IP address and request metadata on our servers).

How we use information

  • Provide and improve the Social Calendar app and API
  • Authenticate you and send account emails (verification, password reset)
  • Store media you upload (such as profile photos) securely
  • Prevent abuse and keep the service reliable

Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We use infrastructure providers (hosting, database, object storage, email delivery) solely to operate the product. They process data under our instructions.

Retention & deletion

We keep your data while your account is active. You can delete your account in the app (Settings → Delete Account). Deletion removes your account and associated data from our primary systems, subject to short backup retention windows.

Security

We use industry-standard practices such as encrypted transport (HTTPS), hashed passwords, and access-controlled storage for uploaded media. No method of transmission or storage is perfectly secure.

Children

Social Calendar is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Your choices

You can update profile information in the app, request support, or delete your account. For privacy questions, contact us via the Support page.

Changes

We may update this policy as the product evolves. We will revise the “Last updated” date above when we do.
